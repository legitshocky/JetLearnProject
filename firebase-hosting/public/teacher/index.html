CCTC (Course Change, Teacher Change) is triggered when a learner moves to the next course requiring a different teacher or kit.

What triggers it:

  • Learner in last 4 classes of current course
  • Next course confirmed in PRM Prep on Athena
  • Migration form submitted on time (after Class 8)

What does NOT trigger it:

  • Submitting after the course ends
  • Not checking PRM Prep first
  • Incorrect JLID format

Always open PRM Prep in Athena first.

  • Current course end date — confirm exact last class date
  • Next 3 courses — understand kit requirements
  • Your upskilling status — check before selecting in form
⚠️Skipping PRM Prep causes cascading errors — kit delivery, teacher assignment, and certificate timing all break.
