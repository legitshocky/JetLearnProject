CCTC (Course Change, Teacher Change) is triggered when a learner completes their current course and moves to the next course — and the next course requires either a different teacher or a course kit.

What triggers it:

  • Learner is in their last 4 classes of the current course
  • Next course is confirmed in PRM Prep on Athena
  • You submit the migration form on time (after Class 8)

What does NOT trigger it:

  • Submitting after the course has ended (too late)
  • Submitting without checking PRM Prep first
  • Incorrect JLID format blocking the HubSpot workflow

Always open PRM Prep in Athena first. This single step prevents most errors.

  • Current course end date — confirm the exact last class date to avoid premature or late submissions
  • Next 3 courses — review the learner's upcoming course sequence to understand kit requirements
  • Your upskilling status — check your training for upcoming courses before selecting in the form
⚠️ Skipping PRM Prep causes cascading errors across kit delivery, teacher assignment, and certificate timing.
Incorrect JLID format (brackets, spaces, symbols)
Wrong last class date — not verifying PRM Prep first
Late submission — filling after the 4-week window
Wrong migration reason — selecting incorrect category
Skipping Athena check before submission
Selecting wrong upskilling level for next course

Each of these mistakes requires manual intervention by the ops team, delays kit delivery, and can result in a learner having no teacher on Day 1 of their next course.

Fill the Last Class Form immediately after the final class of the current course — if you are upskilled and will continue teaching the learner.

What happens next:

  • Form triggers certificate generation
  • Certificate is processed and sent
  • Learner receives it on Day 1 of the next course

Perfect timing means the certificate arrival coincides with the new course start — celebrating completion and building excitement for the learner.

  • ✅ Verified PRM Prep in Athena — know the last class date and next courses
  • ✅ Using exact JLID format — no brackets, spaces, or symbols
  • ✅ Submitting at least 4 weeks early — critical for kit-based courses
  • ✅ Selected correct upskilling status — "Not Onboarded" if untrained
  • ✅ Confirmed migration reason matches the actual situation
